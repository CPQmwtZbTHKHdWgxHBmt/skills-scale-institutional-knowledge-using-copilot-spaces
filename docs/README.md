# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory centralizes the project management processes OctoAcme follows to define, plan, execute, and improve work across cross-functional initiatives.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme uses a lightweight lifecycle that begins with project initiation and stakeholder alignment, moves into planning and backlog definition, and then focuses on disciplined execution with tracking, quality checks, and risk management. After delivery, the team releases changes, communicates outcomes, and closes the cycle with retrospectives and continuous improvement.

## Lifecycle at a glance

1. Initiation: validate the problem, confirm stakeholders, define success metrics, and decide whether to proceed.
2. Planning: shape scope, backlog, milestones, dependencies, and ownership.
3. Execution: deliver in small increments, manage blockers, and maintain quality through reviews and testing.
4. Release & Deployment: validate the release, communicate changes, and monitor outcomes.
5. Retrospective & Improvement: capture lessons learned and turn them into action items.
